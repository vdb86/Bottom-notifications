# Release notes

What changed in each version of [Bottom notifications](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bottomnotifications.app), newest first. The same text as the app's What's new page.

## 2.8

- Several Pro features are now free.
- New: lock the floating button's position, and choose how long to hold it before it moves (Button tab, Position card).
- New with Pro: Screen lighting on the lock screen too (Lock screen card), and any of its settings can be different there. A notification that wakes the screen lights it, and unlocking carries the light on to the home screen.
- The lock-screen button (Pro) now has the floating button's gestures and Action wheel. Actions that open something ask you to unlock first, and any gesture can be different on the lock screen.
- The lock-screen button (Pro) now plays the floating button's appear and hide animation, also each time the screen turns on, and the animation can be different on the lock screen. Unlocking swaps it for the floating button without a blink.
- Fixed the shade's newest notification sometimes sitting below the bottom edge, and the list creeping up by itself after opening or after swiping a notification away.
- The floating button answers a tap at once, also while notifications are arriving and Screen lighting plays. (A tap used to wait a moment in case a second tap followed; two quick taps were lost.)
- Fixed the lock-screen button (Pro) sometimes being invisible after the screen turned on, although it still worked when tapped.

## 2.7

- New with Pro: the floating button on the lock screen too. Tap it to open the shade, drag or fling it to move it. It shares the floating button's position and settings, and any setting can be different on the lock screen. Needs the accessibility service.
- Omnideck actions: with several Decks in Omnideck 3.3 (Pro), choose which Deck an Open or Show / hide Deck action opens. Its name stays up to date when Decks are renumbered in Omnideck.
- Swiping notifications away is easier: new Diagonal swipes and Dismiss cue switches on the Shade tab, and Advanced swipe settings go lower.
- Fixed an empty notification sometimes staying in the shade after dismissing an app's last notification.

## 2.6.1

- Fixed the list widget showing icons and pictures on the wrong notification after dismissing one.
- Fixed the floating button moving to the middle of the screen or off it when folding or unfolding a foldable phone.
- Fixed the floating button hiding behind the navigation buttons when snapped to the bottom of the screen.

## 2.6

- Edge swipe: give each strip its own touch area (position and width), and tap a strip to run an action or open the Action wheel. The Action wheel can also open at a fixed spot instead of where you touch, and has a new Opacity setting.
- New optional buttons below a notification: Copy code, Open link, Call and Email appear when the notification contains one of those. Switch each one on in Buttons below the notification on the Shade tab. The Actions button now finds email addresses too.

## 2.5

- Pinning and protecting are now separate: pin a notification at the top or the bottom of the shade, and protect a notification or a whole app so it can't be swiped away or cleared by Clear all. Small icons on the row show what is pinned or protected, and Notification manager rules can pin, unpin, protect and unprotect.
- Apps pinned to the top now stay at the top of the shade and the list widget when newer notifications are at the top.
- New buttons at the bottom of the shade: Clear all and Settings, plus Release held, Do Not Disturb and Snooze all with Pro. Choose which ones show and their order on the Shade tab.
- Gestures are simpler: each button now has one set of gestures, a tap plus either directional swipes or an Action wheel, edited right on the Button tab. Gesture modes are gone; your default mode's gestures were kept.
- New Edge swipe tab: invisible strips on the screen edges. Swipe in from an edge to open the shade, or give each direction its own action or an Action wheel. "Show floating button" moved to the top of the Button tab.
- New "Hide when the shade opens" switch on the Button tab: the button steps aside while the shade is open. Free for everyone.
- Edge swipe: give each strip its own actions, or let both share one set with Mirror, for directional swipes and the Action wheel. The wheel editor shows the half wheel on its real screen edge.
- Fixed the button sometimes not coming back after unlocking the phone, which could also block taps in the next app.
- The long-press menu on a notification stays inside its panel and shows when there are more options to scroll to.
- New "Omnideck action..." for every gesture, Action wheel slot and Edge swipe: pick any action in Omnideck (an app's screen, a link, a website, a saved text, a web request, a Deck trigger and more), give it a name and an icon, and Omnideck runs it. Needs Omnideck 3.0.
- New Animals page on the General tab: a little pixel-art dog or cat lives at the bottom of your screen. It walks, sniffs, plays and naps, and taps go right through it. The Labrador and the House Cat are free; 15 more dog breeds, 18 more cats, size, energy, when it comes and goes, and a mode where it runs in and barks or meows when a notification arrives come with Pro.
- Uses much less memory while running in the background.

## 2.4

- Added 7 subtler effects around the button: Swell, Fireflies, Raindrops, Wobble, Gyro, Drizzle and Fringe.
- Screen lighting now lights up only for notifications that alert you. Silent, ongoing and progress notifications don't, and the light fades out when its notification goes away.
- Replying from the shade opens the keyboard by itself, and the notification you're replying to stays visible above it.
- Fixed the effect around the button playing below the button on phones with on-screen navigation buttons.
- In the centred browsing modes, a short swipe now moves to the next notification, and notifications settle into place faster.
- Fixed taps on notifications sometimes doing nothing in the centred browsing modes until you scrolled.
- Fixed the shade's media controls stopping working after the player restarted.

## 2.3.1

- Fixed taps near the button not reaching the app below while Screen lighting or the button's appear and hide animation played.

## 2.3

- Added Screen lighting: the screen lights up when a new notification arrives. Pick a screen border effect and an effect around the button, and they play together.
- Added 10 screen border effects and 13 effects around the button that follow its shape, with colour, width, transparency and duration options.
- Added a Screen lighting action to the Notification manager, so a rule can light up in its own style.
- Added the Action wheel: swipe from the button to open a wheel of actions around it, then slide onto one and lift. Each gesture mode can use its own wheel.
- Added Previous app, Assistant, Torch and Toggle mute actions.
- Added animations for the button while media plays, such as Record, Tape and CD, with their own colour.
- The button can now appear and hide with any of the 36 shade animations. Use Try it to see it on the button.
- Added a Text tab to app and custom actions: save texts and paste them with a gesture.
- Added a Test button to Add action's activities and the intent editor. Screens that other apps can't open are now marked.
- Added icon pack support, and your own icon for any app.
- Lists and menus now show when there is more to scroll.
- Add action opens faster, and its tabs no longer get stuck between two pages.
- Menus can no longer be closed by an accidental swipe.
- Clearer messages for backup, restore and update checks.
